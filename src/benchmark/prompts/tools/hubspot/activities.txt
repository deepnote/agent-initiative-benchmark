Read activities linked to a readable CRM record. Defaults return 20 compact rows, newest first, with up to 800 body characters each. body_truncated marks shortened text. Compact rows omit metadata_json, created_at and updated_at. Use activity_id for the full exact activity, or detail=full for full rows. body_chars sets the compact body limit from 0 to 4000. query filters a literal substring in title or body, and activity_type filters the native type. Totals and paging reflect these filters after parent access checks. order is desc or asc. start_time and end_time are inclusive timestamps with time zones. Follow paging.next.after with the same filters and projection. Missing or shortened text is not evidence of absence.
