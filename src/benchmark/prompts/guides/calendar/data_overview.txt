Calendar contains calendars and events with agendas, time zones, organizers, attendees, responses, reminders, recurrence and cancellations. Use calendar_list_calendars, calendar_search_events, calendar_get_event and calendar_query_free_busy. Private detail can be redacted by access. Daily/weekly recurrence and stored exceptions are supported within limits. Other recurrence can fail. Search is bounded to 366 days and free/busy to 93 days. All-day end dates are exclusive. Creation/update times describe edits, not a full edit log. Calendar ACLs and users are stored without separate catalog tools. Follow stored links to meeting notes only through available tools.
