Stripe contains customers, products, prices, subscriptions, invoices, payments, refunds and billing events for the configured account. Use stripe_search_customers and the subscription, invoice, payment and event list tools. Use stripe_list_objects for standalone products, prices and refunds, and stripe_get_object for exact records. Lists have cursors. Money uses integer minor units and each record's currency. Keep currencies separate. Payment, invoice, subscription and refund statuses describe different objects. Events preserve historical payloads and event times. Read current objects separately when needed. Hosted invoice URLs are links, not a document-fetch capability.
