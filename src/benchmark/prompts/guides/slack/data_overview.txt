Slack contains channels, employees and dated messages about company, team, project, customer and operational work. Use slack_list_channels and slack_list_users for discovery, slack_search_messages for recent or older discussion, and slack_get_thread for full conversation pages. Messages can include blocks, edits, reactions and file metadata. Keep their authors, times and thread context. Follow returned cursors. Employee conversations in DMs and group DMs are excluded. Files have stored schemas, but no separate file-content tool is exposed. A pasted link does not make its target readable.
