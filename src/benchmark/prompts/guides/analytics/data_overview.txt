Google Analytics contains property settings, dimension/metric definitions, traffic and event rows, acquisition sources, geography, devices, engagement, key events and stored revenue fields. Version 2 also exposes website/page/campaign catalogs, audiences, saved realtime minutes, weekly cohorts, attribution paths with fractional credit and named models, and session/event samples. Availability depends on retained files. Samples do not establish totals. Start with analytics_get_metadata, use analytics_run_report for supported reports and analytics_read_rows for stored records. Dates use the fixed task snapshot and property timezone. Saved realtime is historical evidence. Cross-bucket unique-user totals, cross-bucket event-grain session totals and user key-event rate are unsupported without the required identities or numerators. Preserve rate denominators, privacy, sampling metadata and business source/medium values. Missing projections and unsupported calculations are explicit gaps.
