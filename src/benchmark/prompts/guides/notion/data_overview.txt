Notion contains policies, plans, decisions, handbooks, meeting notes and database trackers. Pages have full Markdown bodies, typed properties, parents and edit times. Use notion_list_catalog for page/database discovery, notion_search for ranked semantic matches, notion_fetch_page for bodies and notion_query_database for rows. Use notion_list_history and notion_fetch_version for stored revisions and older bodies. Follow body cursors before treating text as complete. Search is a ranked subset, not a complete listing. History exists only where stored. Calendar links and page relations connect context. No general attachment reader is provided.
